AUDIT TYPE: IC-VALUE — TWO CLAIMS ABOUT THE SAME QUANTITY

planned_change
Both original and planned competing claims must remain about the SAME intended
entity, quantity, period, unit and aggregation scope, not just expected digits.
Different periods, additional income, separate products or a new measurement
basis may make the claims compatible, failing the intended conflict. Check
references in the actual before/after order; position does not make a claim correct.

unintended_change
Reject unauthorized changes to other evidence or question meaning, unplanned
numbers (including number words), source/credibility cues, answer hints,
construction tags or corrections/retractions discarding a claim. Authorization
comes from authorized_edit_slots, not edits. Added wording can reinterpret even
mechanically unchanged text; the intended competing value itself is authorized.

readability
Require grammatical, understandable wording and connections, with clear quantity
and time references. A first sentence must not depend on a later antecedent such
as "that same quarter". Conflict, having to choose between claims, harmless
formatting or tense alone are not readability failures. Do not select a winning
value or require another answer-generation step.
