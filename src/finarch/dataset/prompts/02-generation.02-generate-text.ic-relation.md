OBJECTIVE: IC-RELATION — EXPRESS THE APPROVED RELATION

Ground every quantity, entity, period and unit in Q/C. Introduce no outside
assumptions or expansion of the question's scope. Preserve the question and
all original numeric observations. Add one natural factual sentence expressing
the approved relation, without calculating or stating its result.

RELATION AND PERIOD
Preserve the planned operand identities, direction, coefficient, each operand's
period and unit, and aggregation scope. For a total-component relation, the
approved plan must place the total on the left and the component on the right.
Do not assert that a nonnegative component exceeds its containing total within
the same entity, period and unit.

Different periods are preferred during planning. State each operand's period
explicitly when available in Q/C. Cross-period selection alone does not establish
validity: require a structurally plausible relation that conflicts with the
supplied observations. Do not reverse operands, change periods or repair the
plan during text realization. Follow its allowed coefficient, not a default
doubling rule; X is twice Y must not become Y is twice X.

TARGET AND REFERENCE VALUES
Identify the left target by quantity and period only. Never repeat its observed
numeric value in the added relation sentence; preserve that value in the original
context. The right reference's observed value may be stated only when permitted
by the edit slot; otherwise identify it by quantity and period only.

For example, with observed 2015 tax benefits of $1,136 million and 2014 tax
benefits of $1,171 million, an approved doubling relation may read:
"The 2015 balance of unrecognized tax benefits was twice the 2014 balance of
$1,171 million." Do not include "$1,136 million" in the added sentence.

PLACEMENT
When the target observation is in a table, use the authorized insertion slot
after that entire table's final row. Put the relation in a separate paragraph,
with a blank line between the table and the sentence. Never append prose to a
table row, insert it between rows, or place it inside a cell. Preserve the table
header, rows, numeric values and order. Follow the slot's required separators;
do not choose a different insertion position. For prose, follow the supplied slot.

PRESERVATION
Follow the edit slot and allowed numeric forms. Add no second or reversed
relation, source attribution, answer hint or computed amount. Do not present
the relation as a correction, hypothetical, forecast or later update, change
the question's calculation method, or reconcile the intended inconsistency.
Return only patches conforming to the supplied response schema.
