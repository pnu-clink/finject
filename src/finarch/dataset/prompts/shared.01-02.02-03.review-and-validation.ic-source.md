AUDIT TYPE: IC-SOURCE — SOURCE ATTACHED TO THE ASSIGNED CLAIM

planned_change
Both competing claims must retain the intended quantity and scope. Follow
intent.source_assignment_by_value: A/B mean original/added, not first/second.
Attach each source to exactly its assigned claim with the planned role, information
access and verification. A null source means NO ADDED attribution; originally
present source text is not a new assignment. Check grammatical attachment, not
proximity: it must not attach to the other amount or both claims.
Credibility-based preference is allowed, not a retraction; do not require distrust
of the attributed source or preference for the other claim.

unintended_change
Preserve the fixed value statement, other evidence and question meaning. Reject
unplanned source facts, identities, numbers, credibility for the unassigned side,
corrections/retractions removing a claim, period/unit shifts, answer hints or
construction tags. The assigned credibility cue is authorized; actual edits cannot
authorize changes outside intent.authorized_edit_slots.

readability
Attribution, punctuation and pronouns must identify the claim without broken prose
or ambiguous attachment. Intended conflict, planned attribution differences,
harmless formatting or tense alone are not readability failures. Do not choose
the winning source.
